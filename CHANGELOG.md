# Changelog

## v1.0 — update (October 2026)

- **Uninstall protection:** still optional. Turning it off now waits a delay you choose — 1, 3, 7 or 30 days. While the protection is on, the delay can only be made longer (a pending stop is pushed back too), and changing the phone's clock doesn't shorten it.
- **“Unlock as soon as all due cards are done”** is now on by default (can be turned off in Settings → Due cards).
- **Automatic start** of the lock stays on by default.
- **Accessibility service turned off:** the warning now comes at every hourly check instead of every 3 hours, and also when Android switches the service off entirely. Setup step 3 has a clear warning explaining how to turn it back on.
- **About page redesigned:** one card with an icon per feature, and an **Ankineer ecosystem** grid (FocusForce, PopEdit, Ankineer AI) — each card opens its video.
- README: Ankineer ecosystem grid with video thumbnails.


## v1.0 — first public release (October 2026)

- Locks the phone until the daily AnkiDroid goal is reached: cards **and** minutes (minimum 50 cards · 15 min), PC reviews included after sync.
- Due-card options (sufficient / required), focus sessions, automatic start once setup is complete.
- Emergencies: code (once a day, 5 attempts) and 5-minute extreme emergency (once a day), protected against clock changes.
- Strict mode (Settings and uninstall blocked while locked) and optional “block uninstalling forever”.
- Success window, hourly reminders with quotes, alert when the service is stopped.
- Statistics: retroactive streaks, today's vs. overall retention, 7-day / 30-day / 1-year charts, activity heatmap, 365-day calendar.
- 4 home-screen widgets × 4 styles, floating progress circle.
- In-app update without uninstalling; copyable crash report; rescue mode.
- French / English, dark / light theme.
